Soothing spacewalk scenes.

Today, August 18, NASA’s @astro_anil and ESA’s @soph_astro completed a 6 hour, 23 minute spacewalk to remove a failed communications antenna and stow it outside the International Space Station. These challenging tasks are accompanied by rewarding views as the orbiting laboratory continues soaring at 17,500 miles per hour over our blue marble below. The next spacewalk is scheduled for Tuesday, August 25.
